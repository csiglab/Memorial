Structural Insight

Pattern Characteristics

  • 01 Institutionally coordinated: DSIR → CRIs → PROs (1926-2025)
  • 02 Sectorally concentrated: Biological production (dairy, meat)
  • 03 Cooperative-structured: Fonterra legacy and farmer-owned institutions

Historical Contrast

Unlike typical industrial powers, NZ's tecnogenesis has never been:

  • Military-industrial driven
  • Heavy-manufacturing driven
  • Capital goods ecosystem driven
