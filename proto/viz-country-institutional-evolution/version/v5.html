The Foundation Era

The establishment of core governmental frameworks following revolutionary transformation. Three branches of government defined, initial cabinet positions created.

1820 — 1865

The Expansion Era

Westward expansion drives institutional growth. New territories require administrative oversight, leading to the creation of territorial offices and expansion of federal reach.

Territorial Management Land Grants Infrastructure

1865 — 1900

The Reconstruction Era

Post-conflict institutional rebuilding. Civil service reforms replace patronage with meritocracy. Regulatory bodies emerge to manage industrialization.

Civil Service Act Regulatory Bodies Merit System

Professionalization

1900 — 1945

The Progressive Era

Response to industrial complexity and economic crisis. Unprecedented expansion of federal authority. Social welfare institutions established.
